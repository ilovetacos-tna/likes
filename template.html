How Compare works
  • Fill in your sheet, click Get links, and send the Share link to your partner.
  • Your partner opens it, fills in their own sheet, and clicks Compare. They can send you the Comparison link so you see the same results.
  • You both want: both of you chose Favorite, Like, or Interested.
  • Worth a conversation: at least one of you chose With discussion, and neither said No.
  • Paired columns match opposite sides: giving with receiving, in charge with following.
  • Items left blank count as No. An unanswered item never shows up as a match.
  • Answers are kept in the link and in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.
